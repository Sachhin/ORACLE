## Subject
Invitation: independent blinded review of a reproducible human-longevity evidence audit

Dear Colleague,

I am seeking independent scientific reviewers for ORACLE, a model-free research and evidence-auditing project examining the question:

“Can we identify previously unknown biological mechanisms involved in human longevity?”

The current review is deliberately narrow. It does not ask reviewers to endorse ORACLE or to validate a claimed discovery. The current evidence includes mixed cross-cohort results for a published mitochondrial ribosomal protein gene signature, exploratory human blood transcriptomic analyses, and several provisional mechanistic hypotheses. ORACLE currently claims no newly validated causal longevity mechanism.

Would you be interested in independently reviewing a frozen, blinded set of research hypotheses and their evidence packets? Relevant expertise includes one or more of:

- human ageing, geroscience, or exceptional longevity
- transcriptomics, statistical genomics, or reproducible bioinformatics
- mitochondrial biology, mitochondrial translation, or ribosome biology
- causal inference and experimental design

## Review process
- At least two reviewers will assess the same frozen corpus independently.
- Packets will omit author/source identifiers where practical; reviewers will not receive the private unblinding key.
- Reviewers will score source traceability, evidence quality, biological plausibility, novelty relative to cited prior art, confounding, statistical support, falsifiability, and experimental feasibility.
- Each score requires a short rationale and source/claim checks.
- Reviewers will declare relevant conflicts of interest before receiving the packet.
- Disagreements will be adjudicated after individual submissions are frozen.
- Public attribution is optional and will require explicit consent.

The expected time commitment and packet size will be confirmed before you accept. No unpublished personal data or clinical identifiers will be included.

If you may be interested, please reply with a short description of your relevant expertise, a public profile or selected publications, and any potential conflicts. Please do not send confidential data.

Thank you for considering this reproducibility effort.

Best regards,
ORACLE independent scientific evaluation
